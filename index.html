Our story

A family, a hot oven, and Grandma Anna Clare’s recipe.

Juliette grew up in upstate New York making popovers with her grandmother, Anna Clare, on weekend mornings. Mustafa came from Turkey with a tea blend of his own. Together they opened Popovers Café on North Salem Street.

Every popover on the board is named after someone in the family — the original for Grandma Anna Clare, the rest for the kids. Their son Landon — a Culinary Institute of America pastry graduate — runs the kitchen; on most mornings you’ll find a few more of the family at the counter.

“It has a warm family meaning for me.”Juliette Ozturk, co-owner
The room

A warm corner of downtown Apex.

Exposed brick, a floral mural the length of the wall, velvet chairs and fresh flowers on the tables. Come for a popover, stay for the second glass of tea.
